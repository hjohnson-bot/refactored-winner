    Action Flags

    Triggered automatically from current period vs prior period & forecast.

      Flag rules:
      • Revenue decline: current period revenue run-rate < prior year revenue
      • Margin compression: gross margin % decline > 1.0pt vs prior year
      • Expense spike: operating expense run-rate > prior year by > 5%
      • Negative cash flow: YTD operating + investing + financing cash < 0
      • Negative cash position: Cash at end of period < 0
      • Forecast miss: forecast net margin % < prior year by > 1.0pt
      • A/R risk: A/R change in cash flow < -$500k (significant uncollected revenue)

      Source: QuickBooks Online (live) · Data snapshot generated · As of

      No hardcoded numbers — every value loads from data/snapshot.json, which is regenerated from QuickBooks via scripts/refresh.sh.
