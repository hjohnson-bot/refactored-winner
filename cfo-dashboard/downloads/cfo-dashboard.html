    By-segment projection (year-end)

    SegmentYTD revenueRun-rate × 12% of total
    Method definitions:
    • Run-rate: YTD ÷ completed months × 12. Naive — assumes no growth and no seasonality.
    • YoY growth-adjusted: Apply prior FY's YoY growth rate to the remaining months.
    • Seasonality-adjusted: YTD actual + prior-year average monthly revenue × remaining months.
    • Pipeline-driven: Use open-invoice billing cadence × remaining months, converted to recognized revenue using the YTD billings-to-revenue ratio. Most CFO-grade because it reflects actual project work.
